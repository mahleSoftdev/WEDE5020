How quickly will someone reply?

Enquiries sent through this form are answered within one working hour between 07:00 and 17:00 on weekdays. Anything sent over a weekend is answered by 09:00 on Monday. Genuine emergencies should always go to the 24-hour line instead.

Do you charge for a quotation?

Written quotations are free. A diagnostic call-out, where a plumber has to find the fault before it can be priced, is charged at R650 and is credited against the repair if you go ahead with us.

Can I send photos?

Yes, and it helps. Send them to info@johnsplumbing.co.za or on WhatsApp to 082 345 6789. A photo of the fitting and one of the whole room is usually enough for us to price the job over the phone.

Which payment methods do you accept?

EFT, card on site, and SnapScan. Accounts are available for landlords and property managers with more than five units. Cash is accepted but we prefer not to carry it.
