Why we photograph every job

A photograph before and after the repair protects both sides. It shows the customer what was actually wrong behind the wall, and it gives us a record if a warranty claim comes back to us a year later.

Insurers accept our photo reports for geyser and burst-pipe claims, which usually shortens the assessment by a week or more.

Want your job on this page?

We only publish photographs when the customer has said yes in writing. If you would rather we did not, just say so when you book — it makes no difference to the price or the guarantee.
