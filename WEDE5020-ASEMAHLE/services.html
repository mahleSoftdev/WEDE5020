What each service covers

Leak detection and repair

From R650 for call-out and diagnosis

A rising water bill with no visible puddle usually means a pressurised line is leaking under a slab or inside a wall. We locate it with acoustic listening equipment and a thermal camera before anything is opened up, so the repair is one small excavation rather than a demolished floor.

Included

  • Pressure test of the incoming supply
  • Acoustic and thermal location of the leak
  • Written report with photographs for your insurer
  • Repair quote issued before any breaking work starts

Not included

  • Tiling, screeding or plastering to make good
  • Municipal account queries or rebate applications

Geysers and water heating

From R2 400 installation, geyser unit excluded

We supply and install electric, gas and solar geysers, and we replace failed units the same day where stock allows. A geyser replacement is only legal and only insurable if the safety components go in with it, so the drip tray, vacuum breakers, pressure control valve and overflow are quoted as part of the job rather than added later.

Included

  • Removal and disposal of the old unit
  • New drip tray, vacuum breakers and pressure control valve
  • Electrical isolation and reconnection by a qualified hand
  • PIRB Certificate of Compliance lodged for you

Not included

  • The geyser unit itself, which you may supply or buy through us
  • Roof or ceiling repairs caused by the original burst

Blocked drains and sewer lines

From R850 per drain line

Rodding clears a blockage for a week. Jetting clears the pipe wall and keeps it clear for years. We use a high-pressure jetting rig on kitchen, bathroom and main sewer lines, and we run a CCTV camera afterwards so you can see whether the cause was grease, roots or a collapsed section.

Included

  • High-pressure jetting of the affected line
  • CCTV inspection and a copy of the footage
  • Gully and inspection-eye clean-up
  • Written recommendation if the pipe needs replacing

Bathroom and kitchen fittings

From R450 per fitting, or R3 900 per full bathroom

Taps, mixers, toilets, basins, baths, showers, dishwashers and washing machine points. For renovations we work to the tiler's programme so the first fix is in before the walls close and the second fix happens once the tiling is cured.

Included

  • First fix pipework and set-out for the tiler
  • Second fix of all sanitaryware and mixers
  • Pressure and leak test on completion
  • Removal of packaging and old fittings

Gas installations

From R1 950 plus a R750 Certificate of Compliance

Gas hobs, gas geysers and bottle installations, carried out by an SAQCC-registered gas practitioner. A gas Certificate of Compliance is a legal requirement when a property is sold, and most insurers will decline a gas-related claim without one.

Included

  • Regulator, hose and bottle housing to SANS 10087
  • Leak test and appliance commissioning
  • SAQCC Gas Certificate of Compliance

Emergency call-outs

R1 150 after hours, R650 during business hours

Burst pipes, a geyser emptying into a ceiling, or sewage backing up into a shower. The after-hours line is answered by a plumber, not a call centre, and our target is to be on site within 60 minutes anywhere in the standard service area.

Included

  • Attendance and immediate make-safe
  • First hour of labour on site
  • Temporary isolation so you can use the rest of the property

Before we arrive: close the main stopcock at the meter and switch the geyser off at the distribution board.
